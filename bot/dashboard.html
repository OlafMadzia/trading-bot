Strategy league

Every month the bot re-scores all candidate strategies on the last 10 years, using the trading cost it measured from its own fills. It only switches if a challenger beats the current strategy by more than luck (paired t-statistic above 2, better in both halves of the period, no much deeper drawdown).

What the research found

  • Candlestick patterns don't work. 24 patterns across 40 instruments and 26 years: none of the 15 classic reversal patterns predicted what they claim in both decades tested.
  • Machine learning didn't beat simple momentum. A gradient-boosting model retrained monthly on 15 features was significantly worse out-of-sample.
  • Chasing the hottest strategy hurts. The faster a meta-learner shifts money to recent winners, the worse its results.
  • Momentum's edge is risk, not return. Since 2008 it has trailed the S&P 500, but its worst fall was about a third as deep.
